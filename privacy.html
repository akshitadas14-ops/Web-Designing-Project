Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Last updated: March 21, 2026

Overview

FlixVerse is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information. By using FlixVerse, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

  • Account info: name, email,
    and password on registration.
  • Payment info (we do not store full card details).
  • Viewing history and preferences
    for personalisation.
  • Device info: IP address, browser type, OS.
  • Usage data: pages visited and time spent.

How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve the FlixVerse service.
  • To personalise content recommendations.
  • To send service-related notifications.
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your data. We may share it with:

  • Service providers (payment processors,
    cloud hosting).
  • Law enforcement when required by law.
  • Business partners only with your consent.

Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience and analyse usage. You can control them via your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some platform features.

Data Security

We use SSL encryption, secure servers, and access controls to protect your data. No internet transmission is 100% secure — we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

FlixVerse is not for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect their data. Contact us immediately if you believe a child has submitted personal information.

Your Rights

  • Access and review your personal data.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Opt out of marketing communications.
  • Lodge a complaint with a data authority.

Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Reach us at our contact page or email privacy@flixverse.com.
